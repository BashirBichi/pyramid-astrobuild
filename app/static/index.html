Upload a one-band GeoTIFF for an Earth region and a numeric MOLA or LOLA GeoTIFF for the planetary reference. Files are processed in memory and are not stored.

METHOD / AB-01

Evidence-led terrain matching.

We compare five topographic metrics and explicitly exclude absolute elevations because planetary reference datums differ.

01
Extract terrain featuresRelief, elevation spread, slope, and proportion of gentle surfaces
02
Compute a relative indexWeighted mathematical difference, not an engineering suitability score
03
Verify scientific limitationsResolution mismatch and missing coverage can make comparisons unreliable
DEM pixels must represent elevations in meters. Color imagery is not an elevation raster.
LIVE NASA PDS DATA / AB-02

Fetch the public 4 pixels/degree MOLA or LOLA elevation grid from NASA PDS and extract a local profile. If you have selected an Earth GeoTIFF above, we will also produce an experimental Earth-to-planet comparison using the actual NASA planetary data.

SCIENTIFIC RESOURCES / 005

Data Sources.

Trace every scientific input back to its original space-agency dataset or official catalog.

Transparent provenance
Real NASA gridded datasets are retrieved on request, not silently loaded.

MOLA and LOLA PDS numeric grids are fetched when requested; NASADEM granules are discovered through Earthdata CMR. Candidate map positions are approximate region centers. A NASADEM download may require a NASA Earthdata account. No Earth location is certified as an analog.

RESEARCH PROTOCOL

What makes an analog scientifically defensible?

01 · State the hypothesis

Which specific planetary landform and physical feature are you comparing?

02 · Document the input

Capture mission, product identifier, resolution, processing and area of interest.

03 · Normalize the scale

Compare equivalent spatial scales and separate terrain measurements from environmental conditions.

04 · Verify independently

Check topography, geology and environmental constraints with authoritative sources and specialist review.
